⭐ Top 5 must-sees

  1. Markt & Belfry — central square + climbable medieval bell tower (366 steps, panoramic city views). Map →
  2. Canal boat tour — quintessential Bruges from the water, ~30 min, €12. Boards at multiple jetties. Map →
  3. Basilica of the Holy Blood — ornate upper chapel with the famed relic. Free entry, donations welcome. Map →
  4. The Chocolate Line — Dominique Persoone’s wild shop (wasabi, tobacco, lipstick chocolate). Map →
  5. Brouwerij De Halve Maan — last working brewery in the city centre. Tour + Brugse Zot tasting. Map →

💵 Money & Tipping

€ Currency: Euro. Cards widely accepted; some smaller places are cash-only.

🍽️ Restaurants: service is usually included. Rounding up is appreciated; for great service at sit-down dinners, 5–10% is generous.

🍺 Bars & cafés: not expected — leave small change or round up.

🚌 Taxis: round up to the nearest euro.

🏨 Hotel porters: €1–2 per bag.

⭐ Top 5 must-sees

  1. Èze Village — medieval cliff-top village with sweeping sea views and the Jardin Exotique cactus garden. Map →
  2. Vieux Nice (Old Nice) — narrow lanes, Cours Saleya market (flowers Tue–Sun), socca stands. Map →
  3. St-Paul-de-Vence — fortified medieval village + Fondation Maeght modern art (~25 min from Valbonne). Map →
  4. Le Suquet (Cannes Old Town) — climb to the bell tower for sunset views over the bay and Le Croisette. Map →
  5. Antibes & Cap d’Antibes — Picasso Museum, walled old town, plus the cliff walk along the cap. Map →

💵 Money & Tipping

€ Currency: Euro. Cards widely accepted; carry €20–50 cash for markets, taxis & small cafés.

🍽️ Restaurants: «service compris» — service is included by law. Tipping isn't expected, but €1–2 at cafés or 5–10% at nice restaurants for great service is a kind gesture.

🍺 Bars: not expected — leave coins if you wish.

🚌 Taxis: round up or up to 5–10% for good service.

🏡 Hotel porters / housekeeping: €1–2 per bag, €1–2 per day for housekeeping.
